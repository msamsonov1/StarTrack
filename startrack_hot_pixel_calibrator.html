Phase-Space Inversion & Radiation Microphysics

Decoupling True Stellar Kinematics from Lattice Point-Defect Recombination Centers

CMOS active pixel sensors (APS) aboard modern satellites suffer from non-ionizing energy loss (NIEL). Heavy ions and relativistic protons collide with the silicon lattice, displacing atoms and creating mid-gap bulk defect states (Shockley-Read-Hall recombination traps). These manifest as hot pixels that masquerade as faint guide stars, poisoning Wahba’s problem solvers (QUEST, ESOQ) and causing catastrophic attitude runaway.

1. Gyro-Coupled Stellar Centroid Trajectory Kinematics

When the spacecraft undergoes an angular velocity rotation $\boldsymbol{\omega}(t) = [\omega_x, \omega_y, \omega_z]^T$ measured by inertial gyroscopes, a genuine star unit vector $\mathbf{v}_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$ sweeps across focal plane coordinates $\mathbf{x}_i = [u_i, v_i]^T$ with an instantaneous phase angle $\theta_i(t)$:

$$\theta_i(t) = \arctan2\left(v_i(t) - v_0, \, u_i(t) - u_0\right) = \int_{0}^{t} \left(\boldsymbol{\omega}(\tau) \times \mathbf{v}_i(\tau)\right) \cdot \hat{\mathbf{z}} \, d\tau + \theta_0$$

Differentiating yields $\frac{d\theta_i}{dt} \equiv \dot{\theta}_{\text{gyro}}(t)$. Any true celestial body must continuously satisfy the optical flow velocity field $\mathbf{u}_{\text{opt}} = -\boldsymbol{\omega} \times \mathbf{r} - \mathbf{v}_{\text{trans}}$.

Constraint: $\left|\dot{\theta}_{\text{pixel}} - \dot{\theta}_{\text{gyro}}\right| < \epsilon_{\text{threshold}} \implies \text{Real Star}$
2. Radiation Defect Arrhenius Thermokinetics Solid-State

Radiation displacement spikes generate silicon divacancies ($V_2$) and phosphorus-vacancy complexes ($E$-centers) inside the depletion region. Dark current generation rate $\mathcal{I}_{\text{dark}}$ scales with temperature $T$ according to the classical Arrhenius-Shockley-Read-Hall relation:

$$\mathcal{I}_{\text{dark}}(T) = C_0 \cdot T^{3/2} \cdot \exp\left( -\frac{E_{\text{act}}}{k_B T} \right) + \mathcal{I}_{\text{field}}(F, T)$$

Where $E_{\text{act}} \approx 0.63 \text{ eV}$ represents the mid-gap activation energy and $k_B$ is the Boltzmann constant. Ionizing radiation causes an exponential surge in $\mathcal{I}_{\text{dark}}$ without any angular phase translation ($\dot{\theta}_{\text{defect}} = 0$).

Signature: $\frac{\partial \mathcal{I}}{\partial T} \gg 0 \quad \text{while} \quad \frac{d\theta}{dt} \approx 0 \implies \text{Radiation Defect}$
3. Real-Time State Inversion & Masking Filter Estimation

StarTrack constructs a phase-space discriminant metric $\Lambda_k$ for every bright cluster $k$ on the array by correlating temperature fluctuation $\Delta T$ with gyro phase rotation $\Delta \theta$:

$$\Lambda_k = \frac{\left\| \mathbf{x}_k(t) - \mathbf{x}_k(t - \Delta t) \right\|}{\int_{t-\Delta t}^{t} \|\boldsymbol{\omega}(\tau)\| d\tau} \cdot \left[ 1 + \alpha \frac{\mathcal{I}_k(T) - \bar{\mathcal{I}}_{\text{bg}}}{\sigma_{\text{shot}}} \right]^{-1}$$

If $\Lambda_k < \Gamma_{\text{crit}}$ (where $\Gamma_{\text{crit}} = 0.15$), pixel cluster $k$ is marked as anomalous and assigned weight $w_k = 0$ in the Wahba loss function before entering the attitude quaternion solution.

4. Sensor Architecture & Thermal Dissipation Hardware

The focal plane consists of a radiation-tolerant $2048 \times 2048$ pinned photodiode (PPD) CMOS sensor featuring a $5.5\,\mu\text{m}$ pitch and sapphire front window. Thermal transport obeys Fourier’s heat conduction across the thermoelectric cooler (TEC) mount:

$$\rho c_p \frac{\partial T(x,y,t)}{\partial t} = \kappa \nabla^2 T + Q_{\text{electronics}} - \frac{\alpha_{\text{TEC}}}{d_{\text{sub}}} \left(T - T_{\text{sink}}\right)$$
